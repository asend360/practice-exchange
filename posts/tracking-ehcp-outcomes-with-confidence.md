Every Education, Health and Care plan sets out outcomes a young person is working towards. Keeping track of progress against each one, across a whole year and a whole caseload, is one of the hardest parts of SEND leadership. Here's how ASEND360 approaches it.

## Outcomes at the centre

When a pupil's EHCP outcomes are added to ASEND360, they become the anchor for everything else. Each outcome is rated termly as met, partly met or not yet met, so leaders can see at a glance where a pupil is on track and where they need more support.

## Learning plans that feed the outcomes

Outcomes flow into the pupil's Individual Learning Plan as small, measurable steps. As staff record that a step has been achieved — with a note, photo or video as evidence — the outcome rating updates from those steps. There's no separate tracking sheet to keep in line.

## Provision linked to the outcome it serves

Interventions and provision are linked to the outcome or target they're meant to support, with how often they were delivered. When an outcome isn't moving, you can quickly see whether the support was in place as planned, or whether the plan needs to change.

## A clear view across the school

Leaders see the proportion of outcomes met or partly met across the school, by year group, key stage or pupil group, and how that compares with the same point last year. That makes it easier to spot patterns, such as outcomes in one area of need consistently progressing more slowly.

## Annual reviews without the scramble

At review time, ASEND360 drafts the school's contribution from the EHCP Tracker and ILP for staff to edit, keeps professional advice and family and pupil views together, and produces a single annual review pack, with uploaded reports merged in.

## Written by the people who know the child

There's no AI in ASEND360. Plans, reports and reviews are written by the professionals who work with the young person every day — the platform simply makes sure the evidence they need is in one place.

## What it looks like day to day

For a class teacher, the workflow is simple. When a pupil achieves a step in their ILP, the teacher or teaching assistant marks it as achieved and adds a short note, photo or video. That's it. The pupil's outcome rating, the learning journey their parents see in the Family Hub, and the evidence for the annual review all update from that one entry.

For the SENCO, the EHCP Tracker shows every pupil with a plan, their outcomes and where each one stands this term. Filters make it easy to pick out outcomes that are not yet met, reviews that are coming up and pupils whose provision hasn't been delivered as planned.

For senior leaders and governors, the achievement analysis pulls the same information into whole-school headlines: the proportion of EHCP outcomes met or partly met, compared with the same point last year, and broken down by key stage and pupil group. Because it comes from live records, there's no separate spreadsheet to keep up to date before a governors' meeting or an inspection.

## Working with families

Families can see their child's plan and progress through the Family Hub, and their views are captured as part of the record. That makes co-production part of everyday practice rather than something that happens only once a year at the review meeting.

## Getting started

Most schools start by adding current EHCP outcomes from each plan, then building ILP steps from them over the first half-term. From that point, tracking happens as part of normal teaching, and the first termly ratings and annual review packs follow naturally.
